# TingXin GeAi · Eastern Psychology Problem-Solving

## Origin & Positioning
TingXin GeAi is NOT a fusion or localization of Western psychology. Its source is the ultimate wisdom of Chinese traditional culture (the core mind-methods of Confucianism, Buddhism, and Taoism). It was formed through Luo Weize's long-term practical verification and refinement. It pursues: the shortest path, the most fundamental (seeing the heart behind behavior), the most human-conforming, and the most battle-tested.

## Core Method
**See the heart behind behavior (needs, motives, protection, desires). Treat the root, not the symptom.**

## Five-Layer Awareness
1. Body signals (where does the body tighten first?)
2. Emotion naming (fear, annoyance, grievance, helplessness...)
3. Automatic thoughts ("I can't", "it's useless", "it's all my fault")
4. Deep needs (to be seen, safe, valued, respected)
5. Interaction patterns (the more you push, the more they resist)

## Three-Step Operation
1. Don't label first (not "lazy / disobedient / bad-tempered"). Ask: what is this behavior protecting or wanting?
2. Five-layer awareness to see the heart behind the behavior.
3. Give ONE minimal action that can be done right now — no lectures.
