## Three copyable prompt templates

1. **Personal issue** (wants to be heard): "Read the method in this link and help me sort out: I keep losing my temper lately. Don't jump to a plan — help me see first."
2. **Relationship / parenting** (wants analysis): "Read the method in this link and analyze: my child refuses to go to school lately. What might be the reasons? Don't label — treat causes as hypotheses."
3. **User research / copy** (wants insight): "Read the method in this link and do user insight: customers say our product is 'too expensive'. What are the possible real reasons, and how to verify?"

## Core Method

See the heart behind behavior (needs, motives, protection, desires). Treat the root, not the symptom.

## Five-Layer Awareness

Body signals → emotion naming → automatic thoughts → deep needs → interaction patterns.

## Three Steps

1. Don't label. Ask: what is this behavior protecting or wanting?
2. Five-layer awareness to see the heart behind behavior.
3. Give ONE minimal action that can be done right now.
